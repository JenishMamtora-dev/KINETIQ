## Brand & Style

This design system pairs the transaction efficiency and modular scan-ability of a tier-one services marketplace with the visual weight, restraint, and deliberate cadence of an international design biannual or cinematic publication. It speaks directly to top-tier creative talent, design directors, enterprise brand builders, and specialized digital artisans who feel alienated by mass-market, commoditized freelance portals.

### Design Movements & Tone
- **Editorial Precision:** Typographic tension achieved through disciplined contrast—combining structured grotesque hierarchy with expressive, literary serif flourishes.
- **Cinematic Chiaroscuro:** Deliberate transitions between bright, luminous curation canvases (`#FFFFFF` / `#F7F9F8`) and deep, immersive viewport showcases (`#0E1512`). The dark sections frame talent portfolios and masterclasses like projected film cells.
- **Micro-Structured Layouts:** Information architecture uses disciplined hairline borders, monospace metadata callouts, and spacious card compositions to eliminate transactional clutter.

## Colors

The palette balances vivid botanical vitality with architectural dark grounds and quiet, high-legibility neutrals.

### Surface Roles & Application
- **Brand Green Core:** `#16A36A` serves as the primary actionable anchor for conversion paths, verified badges, and active focal points. Hover states transition downward into deep green (`#117A50`).
- **Dark Ground Canvas:** `#0E1512` forms the cinematic presentation mode, hero discovery bands, and full-bleed reel takeovers. Elevated elements within this environment reside on `#15201A` with perimeter delineation via `#29372F`.
- **Light Ground Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` anchors active utility layers, order flows, checkout matrices, and detail pages. `#F7F9F8` acts as an offset canvas for modular grouping, while `#EAF7F0` provides tinted brand recognition for active tabs, status chips, and promotional accents.
- **Contrast Ratios & Typography Tokens:** Text values are locked to specific canvas realities. Light contexts use `#18221D` (primary) and `#66736B` (secondary metadata). Dark contexts rely on `#F5F8F6` (primary) and `#B0BDB5` (muted attributes).
- **Semantics:** Status indicators use purposeful, non-distracting saturation: Success (`#15803D`), Warning (`#D97706`), Error (`#DC2626`), and Info (`#2563EB`).

## Typography

The type system blends structural geometric forms with tactile editorial styling.

### Typographic Hierarchies
- **Structural Display (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean geometry for section headings, service category indices, and commercial titles. Tight tracking gives headlines a confident, architectural posture.
- **Workhorse Body & System Labels (`Inter`):** Selected for high data density, micro-attribute legibility, forms, and analytical tables.
- **Literary Expression (`Newsreader` / Editorial Italic):** Used sparingly to emphasize key conceptual phrases within hero narrative titles, curated staff picks, and testimonial spotlights (e.g., _"Exceptional direction"_, _"Curated world-class talent"_). Always styled in italic.
- **Technical Precision (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to production delivery metrics, turnaround days (`03 DAYS`), rating counts, monetary denominations (`$1,850.00`), and protocol tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt spatial grid configured across a 12-column responsive layout engine.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, maximum bounded container width of 1440px, outer margin of `margin` (`3rem`), and standard gutter of `gutter` (`1.5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, gutter of `1.25rem`, outer canvas margins of `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, gutter of `gutter-mobile` (`1rem`), and margins of `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem`).

### Rhythm & Alignment
Service grids strictly adopt a 4-column span (3 cards per desktop row) for hero packages, shifting to 3-column spans (4 cards per row) for directory browsing. Section padding alternates rhythmically: editorial introductions carry generous spacing (`space-xl` multiplied for hero bands), while marketplace listing grids tighten their item gaps with `space-md` to maintain scannability.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation eschews heavily layered, fuzzy dropshadows in favor of razor-sharp surface contrast, tinted ambient glows, and disciplined structural borders.

### Depth Paradigms
- **Hairline Framing:** Surface separation relies primarily on single-pixel solid borders: `#E3E9E5` on light surfaces and `#29372F` across dark cinematic containers.
- **Ambient Floor Shadows:** When floating elements demand physical separation (such as sticky search filters, modal checkout sheets, and active talent previews), use a dual-layer, green-tinted ambient drop:
  - `0 1px 2px rgba(24, 34, 29, 0.04), 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(17, 122, 80, 0.06)` for light interfaces.
  - `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4), 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)` for dark overlays.
- **Cinematic Translucency:** Floating top navigation and filter pills leverage background blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over 85% alpha surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` on light; `rgba(14, 21, 18, 0.85)` on dark).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural, slightly softened corner profile (`roundedness: 1`). 

### Geometry Rules
- **Base Components (`0.25rem`):** Applied to form text inputs, micro badges, and checkbox frames, ensuring functional precision.
- **Service Cards & Drawers (`0.5rem` / `rounded-lg`):** Delivers a crisp, modern gallery card silhouette without entering playful or casual aesthetics.
- **Media Previews & Modals (`0.75rem` / `rounded-xl`):** Video showreels, lightbox containers, and cinematic talent covers utilize structured framing that matches editorial magazine margins.
- **Search Capsules & Quick Filters:** Feature an exception to the base radius, leveraging explicit full pills (`rounded-full`) to distinctly signify immediate interactive status against rectangular media cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#16A36A`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.25rem`. On hover: `#117A50` with an eased translate transition (`translate-y(-0.5px)`). Typography set to `label-md`.
- **Cinematic Inverted:** Used in dark sections. Background `#F5F8F6`, text `#0E1512`. Hover initiates subtle brand green tinting `#EAF7F0`.
- **Secondary Outlined:** 1px hairline border (`#E3E9E5` on light, `#29372F` on dark), background transparent, text inherits foreground contrast.

### Service & Gig Cards
- **Architecture:** 16:10 aspect ratio media cover pinned to the top, accompanied by author avatar overlap. Body features category eyebrow in `code-badge`, headline in `headline-sm`, delivery duration, starting fee anchored right in monospace notation (`JetBrains Mono`, bold).
- **Surface States:** On `#FFFFFF` in light mode, surrounded by a 1px `#E3E9E5` border. On hover, apply a zero-offset outline accent (`#16A36A`) with a subtle image zoom (`scale(1.02)` within overflow mask).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** Background `#F7F9F8`, border 1px `#E3E9E5`, text `#66736B`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Selected State:** Background `#EAF7F0`, border 1px `#16A36A`, text `#117A50`, accompanied by a minimal check indicator.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Cinematic Global Search:** Large input shell (`56px` height) with `#FFFFFF` background, border 1px `#E3E9E5`, inner placeholder styled in `#66736B`. Focus switches the outline to a crisp 1.5px `#16A36A` ring with zero spatial blur. Monospace shortcut prompt (`⌘K`) anchored at the trailing end.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size `18px x 18px`, border 1.5px `#66736B`, radius `2px` for checkboxes, circular for radios. Checked state fills with `#16A36A` displaying a sharp `#FFFFFF` check mark icon.

### Talent Portfolio Rail (Additional Component)
- Full-bleed media carousel specifically engineered for the `#0E1512` cinematic surface. Cards sit on `#15201A` with `#29372F` borders. Embedded video showreels auto-loop on hover with mute controls, displaying audio frequency badges in `code-badge` styling.
